# Starter Prompt 04 — Vertical Social Cut

- **Use when:** cutting a launch or promo for TikTok / Reels / Shorts.
- **Recommended:** comic multiverse style, 9:16 portrait, 30–60 s, punchy voice.
- **Facts to prepare:** the single message, 2–3 keywords for on-screen stamps,
  brand handle.

```text
Cut a vertical social version with vibe-motion-studio for [PRODUCT/FEATURE].
Style: comic multiverse, 9:16 portrait, about 45 seconds. One message only:
[MESSAGE]. Follow that style's Vertical section (stacked panels, 56-72 px
headlines, max 6 words per scene). Punchy voice, Vietnamese captions.
```

> The agent also understands Vietnamese — write the brief in Vietnamese if you prefer.
