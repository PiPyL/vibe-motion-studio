# Starter Prompt 09 — Brand / Design Announcement

- **Use when:** rebrand, design system or executive B2B announcement.
- **Recommended:** swiss editorial kinetic style, 16:9 landscape, 60–120 s,
  measured voice.
- **Facts to prepare:** what changed and why, 2–3 brand values, launch date.

```text
Announce [REBRAND/RELEASE] with vibe-motion-studio. Style: swiss editorial
kinetic, 16:9, about 75 seconds. Giant typographic statements, blue tape
accents on [VALUE 1], [VALUE 2], colophon outro with date [DATE]. Facts:
[PASTE BRIEF OR LINKS].
```

> The agent also understands Vietnamese — write the brief in Vietnamese if you prefer.
