# Starter Prompt 01 — Product Launch Film

- **Use when:** announcing a new product or a major version.
- **Recommended:** glass keynote style, 16:9 landscape, 120–150 s, English VO +
  Vietnamese captions (or VieNeu-TTS Vietnamese VO when cloud quota is out).
- **Facts to prepare:** product name, 3–5 hero features, 1–2 proof numbers,
  release date, links (docs, repo, landing page).

```text
Make a product launch film with vibe-motion-studio for [PRODUCT], version [X.Y].
Style: glass keynote, 16:9, about 140 seconds. Cover, 3 hero features
([F1], [F2], [F3]), one stats scene ([NUMBER] proof point), calm outro with
release date [DATE]. English voice-over, Vietnamese captions. Facts:
[PASTE RELEASE NOTES OR LINKS].
```

> The agent also understands Vietnamese — write the brief in Vietnamese if you prefer.
