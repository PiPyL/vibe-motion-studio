# Starter Prompt 05 — Product Reveal / Drop Moment
> Requires: vibe-motion-studio ≥ 1.1.0. Re-check this prompt on every minor release.

- **Use when:** the video needs one big reveal beat (drop, launch moment).
- **Recommended:** cyberpunk tactical HUD style, 16:9 landscape, 60–120 s,
  cold tactical voice.
- **Facts to prepare:** what is revealed, the exact reveal word/number,
  2–3 supporting specs.

```text
Build a product reveal video with vibe-motion-studio. Style: cyberpunk tactical
HUD, 16:9, about 90 seconds. Build tension with telemetry panels, then drop on
the word "[REVEAL WORD]" with alert banner and meter spike. Specs to show:
[SPEC 1], [SPEC 2]. Facts: [PASTE LINKS].
```

> The agent also understands Vietnamese — write the brief in Vietnamese if you prefer.
